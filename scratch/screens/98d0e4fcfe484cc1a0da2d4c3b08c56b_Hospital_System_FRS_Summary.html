# Hospital Bed & Resource Management System - Functional Requirements Specification (FRS) ## 1. Introduction The Hospital Bed & Resource Management System is a web-based application to automate hospital bed allocation, patient admissions, resource tracking, staff scheduling, and reporting. ## 2. User Roles - **Super Admin:** System monitoring, hospital/user management. - **Hospital Admin:** Bed, staff, and resource management. - **Doctor:** Patient monitoring, bed transfer requests, ICU monitoring. - **Nurse:** Bed status updates, patient assignment. - **Receptionist:** Patient registration, admissions, appointments. - **Inventory Manager:** Oxygen, medicine, and equipment tracking. - **Emergency Staff:** Emergency bed reservation, ambulance handling. - **Public User:** Bed availability search. ## 3. Key Modules & Features 1. **Authentication:** Login, registration, RBAC. 2. **Patient Registration:** ID generation, history management. 3. **Bed Management:** Real-time availability, ICU/General allocation, transfers. 4. **Resource Allocation:** Ventilators, oxygen cylinders, ambulances. 5. **Doctor & Staff Management:** Duty allocation, shift management. 6. **Appointments & Admissions:** Booking, emergency handling, queue management. 7. **Emergency Management:** Rapid resource assignment, priority handling. 8. **Dashboard & Monitoring:** Live statistics, occupancy charts. 9. **Notifications:** SMS/Email alerts for availability and emergencies. 10. **Analytics & Reporting:** Exportable PDF/Excel reports on usage and occupancy. ## 4. Technical Specs - **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap 5, JavaScript. - **Backend:** PHP, MySQL. - **Target Device:** Desktop.
