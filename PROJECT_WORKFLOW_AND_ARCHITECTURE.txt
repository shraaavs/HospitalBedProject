========================================================================================================================
                                     MEDIFLOW - HOSPITAL & BED MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
                                       ARCHITECTURE, WORKFLOW & MODULE SPECIFICATION
========================================================================================================================

PROJECT OVERVIEW:
MediFlow is a real-time, multi-tenant capable, role-based Hospital & Inpatient Bed Management System designed to govern
the end-to-end patient lifecycle: Registration, Triage & Queueing, Bed Allocation & Transfer, Clinical Consultations,
Nursing Vitals & Medication Execution, Emergency Escalations, Inventory Tracking, Billing, and Automated Discharge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PROGRAMMING LANGUAGES & TECHNOLOGY STACK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. FRONTEND LAYER:
   • Programming Language: JavaScript (ES6+ / JSX), HTML5, CSS3
   • Framework & Bundler: React 19 + Vite
   • Routing & Navigation: React Router DOM (v7, HashRouter)
   • Styling & UI Engine: Tailwind CSS v4 + Material Symbols Icons & Glassmorphic Custom Theme
   • HTTP Client: Axios with automatic JWT Authorization interceptors
   • Alerting & Feedback: SweetAlert2 (Popups, Confirmation Dialogs, Toast Alerts)
   • Reporting & Exports: jsPDF, jsPDF-AutoTable (Clinical Discharge Summaries & Invoices), XLSX (Excel Data Sheets)

B. BACKEND API LAYER:
   • Runtime: Node.js (v20+)
   • Web Framework: Express.js (v5)
   • Authentication & Cryptography: JSON Web Tokens (JWT) + Bcrypt.js (10-round salted password hashing)
   • Database Driver & ORM: Mongoose (v9) & Official MongoDB Native Driver (v7)
   • Environment Config: Dotenv

C. DATABASE & INFRASTRUCTURE:
   • Primary Database: MongoDB Atlas (Cloud NoSQL Document Database)
   • Local / Memory Support: mongodb-memory-server (for isolated local testing)
   • Architecture Pattern: RESTful API Micro-routing with Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Middleware

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & ROLE BOUNDARIES (RBAC)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The application is structured into 4 strict clinical and administrative operational roles:

1. 👑 HOSPITAL ADMINISTRATOR (Admin):
   • Purpose: Central governance, system configuration, user provisioning, audit logs, and hospital metrics.
   • Permissions: Full CRUD over Staff Accounts, Hospital Wards, Bed Inventory, Pricing, Analytics & Billing.

2. 🩺 CONSULTING PHYSICIAN (Doctor):
   • Purpose: Outpatient consultations, inpatient clinical reviews, diagnoses, prescriptions, and bed admission orders.
   • Permissions: Access to assigned patients, vitals review, admission request creation, medical dossiers.

3. 💉 STAFF NURSE (Nurse):
   • Purpose: Floor bed monitoring, real-time vital loggings, doctor instructions execution, drug administration.
   • Permissions: Inpatient observations, vitals charts, emergency alarms, bed transfer requests.

4. 🏢 FRONT DESK & TRIAGE (Receptionist):
   • Purpose: Patient intake/OPD registration, doctor appointment bookings, OPD queue management, check-in.
   • Permissions: Patient registration, bed availability lookup, appointment scheduling, discharge assistance.

3. END-TO-END PATIENT LIFECYCLE & CORE WORKFLOWS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WORKFLOW 1: PATIENT REGISTRATION & OPD APPOINTMENT BOOKING
1. Patient arrives at hospital reception or registers online.
2. Receptionist opens "Patient Registration" / "Appointments Queue":
   - Enters demographics: Name, Age, Gender, Contact, Blood Group, Medical Allergies.
   - Generates unique Patient ID (e.g. `P10001` or `PM-445890`).
3. Selects consulting doctor, department (e.g., Cardiology, General Medicine), and time slot.
4. Patient is queued in MongoDB Appointments collection with status: `Scheduled` -> `Checked In` -> `Waiting`.

WORKFLOW 2: CLINICAL CONSULTATION & ADMISSION BED REQUEST
1. Doctor opens the "Appointments Queue" or "My Patients" view.
2. Clicks on patient card to open the "Patient Medical Dossier" modal (views clinical history & past records).
3. Changes appointment status to `In Consultation` and conducts examination.
4. Doctor enters Clinical Notes:
   - Diagnosis & Chief Complaint.
   - Treatment Plan & Digital Prescriptions (Dosage, Frequency, Duration).
5. IF PATIENT REQUIRES HOSPITALIZATION:
   - Doctor creates an "Admission & Bed Request":
     * Selects Ward Type (ICU, General Ward, Emergency, Oxygen Bed).
     * Sets Urgency Priority (Routine, Urgent, Stat Critical).
   - The request emits to the Bed Management Queue and triggers an automated notification to Nurses & Admins.
6. Doctor finalizes consultation -> Status becomes `Completed` -> Saves to `MedicalRecord` collection.

WORKFLOW 3: REAL-TIME BED OCCUPANCY, ADMISSION & WARD MONITORING
1. Ward Nurse / Admin monitors "Bed Management Real-Time Status" dashboard.
2. System displays interactive visual bed cards with color-coded status:
   - 🟢 Available
   - 🔴 Occupied (with patient badge & admission timestamp)
   - 🟡 Cleaning / Sanitizing
   - 🔵 Reserved
   - 🟠 Under Maintenance
3. Nurse reviews incoming Admission Bed Request -> Selects an available bed -> Confirms Assignment:
   - Patient status switches to `Inpatient`.
   - Bed status flips to `Occupied` with link to `patientId`.
   - Real-time hospital metrics update across total available vs occupied counts.

WORKFLOW 4: INPATIENT NURSING CARE, VITALS & MEDICATION TRACKING
1. Nurse logs into Nurse Portal and sees assigned ward inpatients.
2. Regular Vital Recording ("Patient Vitals" / "Nursing Observations"):
   - Logs Heart Rate, Blood Pressure (Systolic/Diastolic), SpO2 %, Temperature, Respiratory Rate, and Consciousness (GCS).
   - System auto-flags abnormal vitals (e.g. SpO2 < 90% triggers high alert badge).
3. Doctor Instructions & Medication Administration:
   - Doctor issues order in "Doctor Instructions".
   - Nurse reviews pending medications and clicks "Administer Dose":
     * Records exact timestamp, nurse ID, and clinical remarks.
     * Prevents duplicate medication dosage.

WORKFLOW 5: EMERGENCY TRIAGE & CRITICAL CARE ESCALATION
1. Emergency patient arrives via Ambulance or critical inpatient deterioration occurs.
2. Receptionist / Nurse opens "Emergency Management Rapid Response Center":
   - Inputs Triage Category (Red - Immediate / Yellow - Urgent / Green - Standard).
   - Triggers Emergency Critical Bed allocation (ICU / Ventilator).
3. An emergency broadcast notification (`SystemNotificationsAlertsControl`) flashes across all active doctor & nurse dashboards.

WORKFLOW 6: INVENTORY & MEDICAL RESOURCE ALLOCATION
1. Departments monitor medical supplies (Oxygen Cylinders, Ventilators, Monitors, PPE).
2. If stock drops below threshold (`lowStockThreshold`), item reflects as `Low Stock`.
3. Clinical staff can raise "Resource Requests" -> Admin approves/allocates supplies directly from Admin Portal.

WORKFLOW 7: TRANSFER, BILLING & AUTOMATED DISCHARGE
1. Doctor marks patient as "Ready for Discharge" or orders a ward transfer.
2. Receptionist / Admin reviews "Admin Billing & Discharge":
   - System aggregates total bed stay duration x daily bed tariff.
   - Calculates doctor consultation fees, pharmacy prescriptions, diagnostic tests, and taxes.
   - Generates itemized `DischargeBill` with printable PDF invoice using jsPDF.
3. Upon payment settlement:
   - Patient status switches to `Discharged`.
   - Bed automatically transitions to `Cleaning` status for sanitation.
   - Once sanitized, bed status flips to `Available` for the next patient.

5. MONGODB DATABASE COLLECTIONS & SCHEMAS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• `admins`: Administrator profiles, credentials, access levels.
• `doctors`: Physician details, department, specialization, cabin, duty schedules.
• `nurses`: Nurse details, assigned wards, shift hours, on-duty availability.
• `receptionists`: Front desk staff details, desk location, shift timings.
• `patients`: Demographic info, medical record number (MRN), blood group, contact, current status.
• `beds`: Bed numbers, ward categories, occupancy state (Available/Occupied/Cleaning), tariffs.
• `appointments`: Scheduled consultations, appointment dates, queue statuses, consultation notes.
• `medicalrecords`: Historical clinical diagnoses, treatment history, doctors' remarks.
• `prescriptions`: Medication names, dosages, frequencies, doctor signatures.
• `vitallogs`: Chronological BP, SpO2, pulse, and temperature logs with patient references.
• `doctorinstructions`: Formal clinical orders issued from doctors to nurses.
• `medicationadministrations`: Actual timestamps and nurse validations of drug administration.
• `nursingobservations`: Inpatient nurse shift observations and notes.
• `admissionbedrequests`: Formal bed requisition orders sent from doctors to bed management.
• `transferdischarges`: Bed transfer history, transfer reasons, and discharge clearances.
• `dischargebills`: Itemized billing invoices, room tariffs, pharmacy costs, payment statuses.
• `inventoryitems`: Hospital equipment and pharmaceuticals stock levels and thresholds.
• `resourcerequests`: Requisitions for specialized medical gear (ventilators, oxygen tanks).
• `notifications`: Multi-role priority alerts (Routine, Urgent, Emergency).
